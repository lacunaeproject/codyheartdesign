Card metrics, five ways

The same two cards each time — Matching Tool for the long dollar figure, Design ROI for the wide ranges and the long third label. Only the stats block changes; hover still works everywhere. Resize the window to see each one wrap.

A — Three columns (previous)

How it shipped before. Scannable and symmetric, but the columns force every label to stay short, and three long labels can rag unevenly.

B — Ledger rows (now live)

Each metric on its own line: label left, figure right, hairlines between. Reads like the financial statements the copy keeps invoking, and labels can be as long as they like. Costs vertical space; the cards get taller.

C — One-line strip

The metrics run as a single quiet line under the hairline, middots between pairs, wrapping like prose. The most editorial and the most compact — but the numbers stop being a place the eye can land from across the room.
