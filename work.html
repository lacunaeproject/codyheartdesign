The tools, and the numbers.

Six studies from seven years inside healthcare operations — three at LifeStance, two at Collective Health, and one about what the work was worth.

Tool ConsolidationInventoried 50+ regional intake tools and showed they reduced to six underlying jobs — the spine of the platform roadmap.
2022 – 2023 · Lead designer
Matching ToolFrom a two-state pilot to 99% national adoption. 147 of 178 coordinators rated it “much better” than what they’d been using.
2022 – 2024 · Lead + PO
Care MatchingTurned a black-box matching funnel into a four-gate measurable system across 33 states. Rematches down 24.1%, bookings up 10% YoY.
2024 – present · Lead designer
Collective Health · 2018 – 2021
ClaimsOut-of-network transcription was the slowest screen on the CX floor — twenty minutes a claim, by hand. Rebuilt fast and auditable: 60% less time on task.
2018 – 2021 · Senior designer
BillingMost of a billing system isn’t billing — it’s reconciliation, disputes, and reporting. That adjacent work ate 40% of the team’s manual effort. We automated it away.
2019 – 2021 · Senior designer
Strategy & ROI

2024 – 2025 · Lead / modeler
Experience

Most of my work is internal tools — the software that call-center agents, clinicians, claims adjusters, and billing teams stare at for eight hours a day. It’s where small friction compounds into millions of dollars and thousands of bad appointments.

Jun 2021 – Jun 2024 LifeStance Health

Designer to Sr. Designer · San Francisco — Led end-to-end design of 45+ features across internal tools, member experiences, and platform infrastructure. Cut time on task by 50% in key CX workflows; eliminated 40% of manual billing work.
