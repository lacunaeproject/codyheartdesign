Hi, I’m Cody Heart — a lead product designer
living in Chicago, IL, building tools at LifeStance Health.

My strength is strategy — five-plus years turning LifeStance’s internal tools into measurable, defensible outcomes. Before that, three years rebuilding claims and billing at Collective Health. The work I’m proudest of shows up as a number on a P&L, and every one of those numbers came from sitting with the people behind the workflow. More about me →
